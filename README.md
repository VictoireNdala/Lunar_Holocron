# Lunar_Holocron
An intuitive tool that allows mission planners, educators, and the general public 
to quickly compare different landing sites and dates by visualizing 
the positions of the Sun and Earth relative to the horizon, 
in order to assess energy production potential as well as the possibilities 
for direct communication with Earth.

**Status:** under construction (NASA Space Apps 2026). All data currently shown is **synthetic demonstration data**, not real values.
